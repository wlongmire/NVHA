Launched in 2023, our series of late-night workshops called “The Midnight Notes,” are designed to offer young emerging Camden writers and poets the opportunity to learn with and from like-minded peers. The program financially supports local featured poets who read during the workshops and provides a space to build confidence in the performance aspect of reading poetry in a low-pressure environment.
