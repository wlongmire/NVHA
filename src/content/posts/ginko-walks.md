In each of these hour-long workshops / nature walks,  local poets take us through immersive mindful experiences in places of particular inspiration to them. These walks are modeled on haiku ginkos, a Japanese tradition akin to ‘forest bathing’ –  taking contemplative hikes through nature with the purpose of gathering material for haikus. These events are in-person, but are also live streamed.
