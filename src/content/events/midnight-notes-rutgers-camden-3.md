The Midnight Notes will return to The Writers House at Rutgers-Camden once again, next Tuesday, Aug. 12th, from 7 PM – 9 PM, hosted by poet and storyteller Reet Starwind.

Join us for a poetry workshop for emerging and veteran poets alike as we write on the prompt: “Golden Hour.” Share your work in a low-pressure environment and share/receive some creative writing tips.

Can’t make it in person? Be sure to tune into our host’s IG live @mrstarwind to check in virtually.
