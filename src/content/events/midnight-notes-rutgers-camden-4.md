It’s our last collab of the year as part of the Chancellor’s Year of the Arts, so we’re bringing you not one but TWO featured poets!

On Tuesday, November 18, from 7-9 in the Rutgers-Camden Writers House, [Pauline Monter](https://nightheronbarks.com/fall-2021/pauline-monter/) and [Patrick Rosal](https://www.patrickrosal.com/) will take the mic to inspire us as we write on the theme of roots/ancestry.

We’ll have snacks and writing supplies. Enter through the rear door of the Writers House.

And if you can’t make it, tune in on our host Sir Reet S. Starwind live!
