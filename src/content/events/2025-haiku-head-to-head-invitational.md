Experience the art of haiku like never before in this live, tournament-style poetry competition! Watch poets go head-to-head in three rounds—each verse sharper, braver, and more inspired than the last.

  
December 11  8 PM – 10:30 PM  
Tickets: $5 

This special fundraiser supports free haiku education for youth in **Camden****, NJ** helping children express themselves through poetry, mindfulness, and creativity.

Due to a challenging fundraising year, our goal for this event is to raise **$5,000.** **This** **funding will directly support bringing** **free beginner-level haiku to** **four Camden classrooms at 30 children each (120 kids total!)**. To make this event a success, we’ve secured **eight participating poets**, and we hope you will consider joining us in the audience for this special invitational.

_Featuring:_

-   Christopher Clauss
-   Randy Brooks
-   Jerry Hardesty
-   Billy Tuggle
-   Omari King-Wise
-   Joel Dias-Porter
-   Jeff Hoagland
-   Deborah Elder-Brown
