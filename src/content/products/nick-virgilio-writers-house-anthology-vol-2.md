The year 2020 was tumultuous. Between the pandemic and protests, plus many more life-changing events, there was plenty to write about. This large anthology collects all of the poetry from the Haiku in Action project we launched last year, along with essays from the staff and an introduction to Haiku writing by Tom Painting. In this book you’ll also find poems from our student haiku and senryu contests, as well as writing prompts to inspire you. Don’t miss out on this seminal collection of poetry.
