The is the inaugural volume of poetry from the Nick Virgilio Writers House, Upright Remington Press. In this edition we have chosen to include the Japanese poetic forms: haiku, senryu, and tanka. As Nick once put it, “A haiku is a record of emotion which somehow links human nature to all nature through keenly perceived experience.” The authors we included either wrote their poems at the grand opening of the house or they responded to a request for submissions. The list of contributors includes correspondents with Nick and/or friends of the Nick Virgilio Haiku Association – a great collection of global and local haiku poets. This is Volume 1 of a periodic series.
