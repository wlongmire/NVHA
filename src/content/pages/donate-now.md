The Nick Virgilio Haiku Association is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, and your donation is tax-deductible.

Your support helps us bring poetry, literacy and healing programs to Camden youth and poets around the world. Your contribution also supports local outreach and community efforts at the Nick Virgilio Writers House, inspiring daily life with the mindful presence of poetry.

A special shout-out to supporters who add a little extra to help with processing fees, or who choose bank (ACH) as the donation method.

**To donate by check,** mail it to President, Nick Virgilio Haiku Association, 1924 Mt. Vernon Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130.
