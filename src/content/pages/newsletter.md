## Past issues

- **March 2025.** A recap of NVHA's Literary Landmark win; the graveside ceremony and Midnight Notes promo; the winners of the 2025 Haiku and Senryu Contest; an open call for board members who work or reside in Camden; summer at the Virgilio Writers House.
- **Fall/Winter 2024.** Late-night poetry practice returns to the Writers House, with a partnership with Rutgers University–Camden and the Rutgers Writers House to bring Midnight Notes to campus in 2025.
- **Spring 2019.** Photos from the grand opening of the Writers House, program updates, and commentary on Nick Virgilio's legacy.

Older issues: Spring/Summer 2017, Fall 2008/Winter 2009, Spring/Summer 2008, Fall/Winter 2007.
