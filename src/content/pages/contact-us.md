## Parking and transit

Some street parking is available along Broadway, Ferry Avenue, and Jasper Street. More is at 1645 Ferry Avenue (Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority Building), two blocks from the Writers House. We are directly on the NJ Transit 401, 402, 412, 450, and 457 bus routes, which connect easily to PATCO at or near the Ferry Ave or Freedom Center stops.
