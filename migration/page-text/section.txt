Annual youth contest for grades 7-12 Nicholas A Virgilio haiku and senryu memorial contest Sponsored by the Haiku Society of America On January 20, 2026, submissions will open for the 37 th Annual Nicholas Virgilio Haiku and Senryu Competition for Grades 7-12. NEW DEADLINE THIS YEAR: All entries must be in hand by March 20, 2026. Entries received after that date will not be accepted. Eligibility: Any student in grades 7 through 12 enrolled in school as of September 2025 may enter. Regulations: Each student may submit up to three haiku/senryu Contest submissions must not have been already submitted elsewhere for publication AI generated haiku will not be considered For full guidelines and to view past winners, please visit the Haiku Society of America Virgilio Contest site Enter Now
